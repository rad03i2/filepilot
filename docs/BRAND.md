# FilePilot Brand Guide

## Identity concept — Routeboard

FilePilot is about **routing files safely**, not merely moving them. The identity borrows from navigation boards, route planning, manifests, and lightweight productivity tools.

The visual personality is bright, calm, precise, directional, and productivity-first.

## Logo

Primary asset: <code>assets/filepilot-logo-square.svg</code>.

The logo combines a folder/container silhouette, a routed path with waypoints, and a completion mark. It communicates **files following a planned route** without relying on a simple letter monogram.

### Usage rules

- Prefer Paper or Porcelain backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the mark.
- Do not add heavy metallic or glossy effects.
- Preserve the blue/coral/mint route sequence.
- At small sizes, use the symbol without extra text.

## Hero cover

Primary asset: <code>assets/filepilot-brand-cover.svg</code>.

The cover is brand artwork rather than a screenshot. It visualizes files moving through an inbox, a preview checkpoint, organized category lanes, and a manifest state.

## Typography

Use a system-safe sans-serif stack: Inter, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif.

## Graphic language

Preferred motifs are dotted route lines, rounded file cards, waypoint circles, manifest tags, directional arrows, and generous whitespace.

Avoid cyber/security imagery, surveillance motifs, fake screenshots or statistics, and unsupported “AI organizer” language.

## Voice

Prefer “preview before changing files”, “reversible file organization”, “collision-safe destination naming”, “manifest-based undo”, and “local-first”.

Avoid unsupported claims such as “zero-risk”, “guaranteed recovery”, “AI-powered classification”, or “automatic cloud backup”.

## Distinctiveness

FilePilot is defined by **light editorial surfaces and route-planning graphics**. It should look like a polished productivity utility, not a security product or a generic developer dashboard.
